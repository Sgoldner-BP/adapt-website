The ADAPT Method

Adapt beyond pain

Keep doing what you love, unlimited by pain.

ADAPT is a skills-first way to work through pain, injury, and the weak links that hold a skill back, using the training you already love.

Work with Sam Watch on YouTube
Who it's for

For people who move, and want to keep moving

  • A skill you love is being held back

    A handstand, a kick, a lift, a run, or an everyday task like standing for a while or carrying the groceries upstairs.

  • Pain, an injury, or a weak link is the reason

    It hurts, or the range, strength, control, or confidence isn't there yet.

  • You'd rather keep training

    ADAPT works through the training you already do, instead of asking you to stop everything and wait.

How it works

A quick screen, then five steps

Each step gives you one Resilience Movement, built around your skill. Most people can set up all five in about an hour; after that it's a few minutes a day and a test once a week.

  1. 0

    Pre-ADAPT Screening

    A checklist of warning signs and health questions, so anything that needs a professional's eyes gets them first.

  2. 1

    Assess Painful Skill

    Name the skill that's held back, how much, and your 11/10 goal: the result that would prove you went past your old limits.

  3. 2

    Discover Testing Movement

    Boil the skill down to the small piece that hurts and turn it into a simple test you do once a week, cold.

  4. 3

    Approach Pain-Free Motion and Contraction Couplet

    Move through that position with zero pain, then hold it into and away from the pain. Daily, a few minutes.

  5. 4

    Program Overload Exercise

    Swap one exercise in your workout for a version that improves the same movement without flaring things up.

  6. 5

    Track All five

    Check off the daily movements and log the weekly test. Two weeks shows the trend, so you know what to change next.
